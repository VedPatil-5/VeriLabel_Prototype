# Rules and validation

The prototype references the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. Fixture findings are demonstration content and must be source-reviewed before being presented as statutory guidance.

Authoritative starting points:

- Department of Consumer Affairs: Legal Metrology Act, 2009
- Department of Consumer Affairs: consolidated/amended Packaged Commodities Rules
- Department of Consumer Affairs: Legal Metrology resources and notifications

The officer dashboard and assistant must distinguish source material, guidance, sample data, and AI explanation. Penalties and Mumbai statistics must not be invented. Unsupported amounts are shown as not calculated in the prototype.
